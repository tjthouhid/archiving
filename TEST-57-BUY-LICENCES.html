TEST-57 — Buy licences for one product

Buying from a licence card offers only that product. The general Buy menu still offers every product.

All guides · Jira TEST-57

What changed

On a licence card, Buy licences opens a new order for that product only. Other courses, and virtual classroom or in-person products, are not in the list, and another product cannot be added. After that order, the button returns to Licences.

/buy/licences lists eLearning products only. Buy from the main Buy menu still lists every product and returns to Orders and invoices.
