TEST-57 — Buy licences for one product

Buying from a licence card shows that product as fixed text, with no product list. Buy licences in the menu lists eLearning courses only.

All guides · Jira TEST-57

What changed

On a licence card, Buy opens a new order for that product only. The product name is shown as text. There is no dropdown, and another product cannot be added. After that order, the button returns to Licences.

Buy licences in the left menu lists eLearning courses only. Virtual classroom and in-person products are not in that list. Buy courses still lists those other course formats, and a generic order still returns to Orders and invoices.
