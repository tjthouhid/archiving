TEST-80 — Registration active and inactive tabs

The organisation registrations list separates learners who are still active from learners set as inactive.

All guides · Jira TEST-80

What changed

/registrations/view-registrations has Active and Inactive tabs. A registration follows the learner’s membership in that organisation. Set inactive moves the learner’s registrations to the Inactive tab. Active learners stay on the Active tab.
