TEST-102 — Inactive and archived learners

Setting a learner inactive or archived does not email them. The next time they sign in with the organisation email, they are told what happened, invited to keep a personal learner record, and can ask the organisation owner to reactivate them. Live organisation courses stay closed.

All guides · Jira TEST-102

What changed

An organisation owner can set a learner inactive or archive them without an email going to that learner.

The next sign-in with that organisation email still opens the learner account. A dialog says the organisation owner set the account as inactive or archived. If they have no personal email, it links them to create a personal learner record. Request re-activation emails the organisation owner once. A second press does not send another email.

Live courses on that organisation’s licence show Access denied and cannot be opened. A completed course from that organisation stays on the learner record. An organisation email that was switched off without making the person inactive or archived still cannot sign in.
