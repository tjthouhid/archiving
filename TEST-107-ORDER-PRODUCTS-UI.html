TEST-107 — Order products actions

The order products step no longer puts a large red Remove button under the line, or a blue Add product button between the line and Next.

All guides · Jira TEST-107

What changed

On each product line, Remove is a red link beside the title, for example next to Product #1. + Add product? sits on the same row as Next, in the same orange as Next. Back stays on the left.

An order opened from a licence card still cannot add a second product. Invoice groups keep their own add and remove controls.

Organisation: /billing/orders-invoices/new. IW admin: /admin/orders/add, on the Order Products step.
