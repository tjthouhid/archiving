TEST-85 — Deletion cause

A deletion from the form is labelled User request. The records-policy deletion date is not calculated.

All guides · Jira TEST-85

What changed

When an organisation owner or an IW admin deletes a user, the record stores the cause User request as well as the written reason. The Deleted tab shows a Deletion cause column. Reports can add a Deletion cause column.

The other cause, Information and Records Management policy, is reserved. The ticket does not say what date the six years is counted from, so no automatic deletion and no policy date are applied.
