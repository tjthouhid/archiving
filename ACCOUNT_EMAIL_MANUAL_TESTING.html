2. What is fixed

Fixed

  • IW admin was showing rob.watson@example.com on the JCB row. An organisation row now shows that organisation’s email.
  • The personal email is labelled Personal under the organisation row. It is not treated as the organisation email.
  • An organisation portal shows only its own email. It does not show the other organisation or the personal email. It may show the words “Linked account”.
  • A hand-added X-Admin-Request header no longer reveals linked or personal emails inside an organisation.
  • IW search finds a person by any of their emails. An organisation search does not find them by a personal email or another organisation’s email.
  • Editing the email inside an organisation changes that organisation’s email only. It does not replace the personal primary.
  • An email that already belongs to someone else is refused.
  • After both organisations are archived, the personal account stays on the IW Active list. Organisation is “Personal account”. There is no “Linked to …” line, because the linked organisations are archived.
  • The archived organisation rows stay on the IW Archived tab, each with its own organisation email.
  • Signing in with the primary email does not ask for an organisation, and opens the learner profile.
  • Account settings still lists OCS and JCB as past employers.
  • The completed course stays on the learner profile: IOSH Safety for Executives and Directors. It is under Expired, because the registration ended on 24 June 2026.

Not changed

  • The two organisation accounts were not merged into one user.
  • Archived organisation emails stay unable to sign in.
  • Delete still anonymises. This work does not make a deleted person recoverable.
  • No Workplace DNA score was created. None is stored for either of Rob’s accounts.
