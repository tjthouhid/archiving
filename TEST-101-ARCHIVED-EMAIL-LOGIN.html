TEST-101 — Archived organisation email

A learner can still sign in with an archived organisation email. They are told that email will not be supported going forwards, and their learning stays available.

All guides · Jira TEST-101

What changed

Signing in with an archived organisation email opens the learner account and shows a notice: that email will not be supported going forwards. A personal email, or an email from an organisation they still belong to, does not show the notice.

On Settings → Account, each organisation is labelled Active account, Inactive account, Archived account, or Deleted account.

With no live organisation, the menu lists My learning, My courses, and Certificates, including courses kept on a linked account. The settings cog opens Account instead of an access-denied page.
